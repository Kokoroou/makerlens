Privacy

Your files never leave your device

MakerLens Assets and MakerLens Events read and decrypt your game in your browser. No file, folder name or key is sent anywhere, and your browser enforces it with a Content-Security-Policy. Once the page has loaded it works offline.

The short version

  • Files stay on your device Nothing is uploaded.
  • Anonymous count No cookies, no IP stored.
  • Feedback is optional Sent only when you press.
  • Hosting and contact No request logs.

Your files

  • Nothing is uploaded. The files are only read locally.
  • When you pick a folder, Chrome may say “upload”. That is its standard wording for giving a page access to a folder.
  • A game link comes from a game published with rpgmaker-web-toolkit. It carries its key after the #, which browsers never send to a server.
  • Your browser sends the key only to that game’s own site. The files go from there straight into your browser, not through MakerLens.
  • The Assets | Events switch passes the open folder to the other tool inside your browser. Nothing is sent.
  • The tools only read. Your game is never modified.
  • Your theme, layout choices and whether you answered the feedback card stay in your browser’s local storage, on your device.

Anonymous usage count

Each page load on the MakerLens site sends one request to GoatCounter. A few actions are also counted by name only, at most once per page load.

Counted

  • The page path
  • The referring site and any campaign parameter, such as utm_source
  • How a game was opened (a folder, an archive or a game link) and whether that worked
  • Its engine family, such as MV, MZ or VX Ace
  • Which features were used: the image viewer, the filters, the flow graph or the scene player
  • The feedback card, and switching between Assets and Events

Never collected

  • What was opened, searched or typed
  • Cookies or identifiers
  • Your IP address (it is not stored)
  • Nothing is sent with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or on a local copy.
  • If the counter is blocked, the tools work the same.

Feedback (optional)

Nothing is sent unless you tap a star or press Send.

A message contains

  • The rating
  • The comment you typed
  • Your browser language
  • Roughly how many games you have opened
  • Bug reports only: your browser and system (you can remove it)

Never included

  • File or folder names
  • Game titles
  • Keys
  • The message is stored with its date but no IP address or identifier. It is kept until deleted.
  • Please don’t put personal information in a comment.
  • To get a reply or have a comment removed, open a GitHub issue.

Hosting and contact

  • Like any website, the pages are delivered by a hosting provider. It sees your IP address to deliver them, under its own privacy policy.
  • Request logs for MakerLens are off.
  • MakerLens is a personal open-source project by Trương Tuấn Anh (Kokoroou).
  • The code, including what is sent above, is public on GitHub (Assets, Events).
  • For a privacy question or request, open an issue there.
