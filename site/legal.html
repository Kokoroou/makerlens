This notice applies to MakerLens Assets and MakerLens Events. It states what the Tools do and do not do, to reduce the risk of DMCA or similar complaints for contributors and users.

Definitions. In this notice, “the Tools” means MakerLens Assets and MakerLens Events; “the Maintainer” means the maintainer of the Tools (Kokoroou on GitHub); and “you” means any person who uses the Tools.

What the Tools do

  1. The Tools:

    • read only the files you explicitly select from your local filesystem;
    • reverse the game’s own obfuscation entirely in browser memory, using the key that ships with the game or the archive;
    • show the result through a temporary Blob URL.

  2. The Tools never:

    • upload, store or transmit any file or key to a remote server;
    • collect information about which files or games are opened;
    • modify your game: they only read, and MakerLens Events is read-only too.
  3. The only automatic network requests are described on the privacy page.
  4. RPG Maker XP, VX and VX Ace. Assets are packed into an archive (.rgssad, .rgss2a, .rgss3a). Its key is embedded in the archive itself.
  5. RPG Maker MV and MZ. The engine stores an XOR key in plaintext in data/System.json, which ships with every game deployment. The Tools read it from your local filesystem and reverse the XOR in browser memory.
  6. Because the keys are co-located with, or embedded in, the content they “protect”, this obfuscation is not considered effective access control under most legal interpretations, and is substantially different from true DRM systems.

Intended use

  1. The Tools are intended for:

    • game developers working on their own RPG Maker projects who need to inspect or recover their own assets;
    • artists and contributors who created assets for a game and need to review or retrieve their own work;
    • modders operating under explicit permission from the game’s copyright holder;
    • researchers and educators studying file formats or game development workflows.

Not for

  1. The Tools are not for:

    • extracting, distributing or repurposing assets from games you do not own or have not received explicit permission to modify;
    • circumventing access controls on commercially distributed games without authorization;
    • any use that violates the End User License Agreement of the game or the RPG Maker engine.

Your responsibility

  1. By using the Tools, you accept that:

    • you are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to access and process the files you select;
    • the Tools do not grant you any rights over third-party copyrighted content;
    • the authors of the Tools are not liable for how you use the output.

Copyright and licences

  2. Decrypting assets for personal inspection does not create a licence to redistribute them.
  3. Kadokawa / Gotcha Gotcha Games publish a separate EULA for each RPG Maker version. You should consult the one that applies to the game you are working with. Common restrictions include limits on asset redistribution and derivative works.

  6. The projects ask contributors not to commit real game assets or game data, even as test fixtures, without permission from the copyright holder.

Takedown requests

  1. If you are a rights holder and believe a Tool is being used to infringe your copyright, please open an issue or contact the Maintainer directly.
  2. The Maintainer will respond promptly.
  3. A takedown request directed at a repository must target the Tool itself, not individual users’ actions, which are outside the project’s control.
